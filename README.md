### ⚡ About Me

I am an engineering student and digital hardware designer specializing in **RTL Design, Microarchitecture, and Digital VLSI Systems**. My work focuses on building digital hardware from first principles—spanning cycle-accurate Verilog modeling, custom processor architectures, physical CMOS layouts, and hardware-software co-design.

- 🔬 **Primary Focus:** Synthesizable Verilog HDL, digital state machines, priority arbitration, and processor architectures.
- ⚙️ **Current Projects:** Multi-domain hardware schedulers, custom 8-bit computers, and pipelined RISC-V cores.
- 🎯 **Objective:** Pushing high-performance, area-efficient, and low-latency digital circuits for ASIC and FPGA targets.
